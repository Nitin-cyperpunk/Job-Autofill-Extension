## Summary

JobFill keeps your job-application profile in your browser on your device. We don't run servers
for JobFill, and we don't collect, sell or share your data. The one feature that sends anything
off your device is the optional AI answer drafting. It is off by default, and it only sends what
you approve, to the AI provider you choose, when you click Generate.

## What JobFill stores

JobFill stores the following in your browser's extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`) on
your device. It is not synced to your Google account and never sent to us.

- **Your profile:** name, contact details, location, work and education history, projects,
  certifications, skills, languages, links (for example LinkedIn, GitHub, portfolio, X, a resume
  link) and answers to common application questions you choose to save.
- **Your resume file**, if you choose to keep it, so JobFill can attach it to application forms.
- **Settings**, for example preview mode and debug mode.
- **AI settings, if you turn AI on:** the provider, the model and your own API key.

JobFill does not keep a history of the sites you visit or the applications you fill, and it
doesn't keep the AI prompts or answers.

## How JobFill uses web pages

JobFill runs on web pages so it can find application forms. It reads form fields and their labels
on the page you're on to work out what each field asks for. When you click **Autofill**, it types
the matching values from your profile into the form. Page content is not stored or sent anywhere.

Once a value is in a form field, the website can read it, just as if you had typed it. Some
sites save drafts before you submit. The same applies to your resume file: when JobFill attaches
it to an application's upload field, that site receives the file just as if you had attached it. JobFill never submits a form for you. Use **Preview fields
before filling** to approve each value first.

## Resume import

When you import a resume (PDF, DOCX or text), it is read inside the extension on your device.
Reading it doesn't upload it anywhere. You review what was found before anything is saved to your
profile.

## Optional AI-drafted answers

This feature is **off by default**. To use it, you turn it on and enter your own API key for
OpenAI, Google Gemini or an OpenAI-compatible service you choose.

- **When data is sent:** only when you click **Generate with AI** for a question, review the
  consent screen, and confirm. Nothing is sent automatically or in the background.
- **What is sent:** the question, plus the items you leave ticked on the consent screen, such as
  the job title, company and job description from the page, and relevant parts of your
  experience, skills and education. You can see the exact text before sending and untick
  anything.
- **What is never sent:** your name, email, phone, address, links, salary expectations,
  work-authorization answers, demographic answers and your resume file.
- **Where it goes:** directly from the extension in your browser to the provider you chose. The
  consent screen names it every time. The provider's own privacy policy and data-retention terms
  apply to what you send it. It also sees your IP address, as any website does.
- **Your API key** is stored only in your browser's extension storage and is sent only to its
  provider. Web pages can't read it.

## What we don't do

- We don't sell, rent or transfer your data to third parties.
- We don't use your data for advertising, for creditworthiness or lending decisions, or for any
  purpose unrelated to filling in your job applications.
- We don't use analytics, tracking, cookies or crash reporting in the extension.
- We don't have accounts, and we don't receive your data on any server.

JobFill's use of information complies with the
[Chrome Web Store User Data Policy](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/user-data-faq),
including the Limited Use requirements.

## Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays in your browser until you delete it or uninstall JobFill. You can:

- **Export** your profile to a file, and **import** it in another browser;
- **Turn off AI and forget the API key**;
- **Delete all local data**, which erases your profile, resume, settings and AI settings.

These controls are on the extension's profile page and its Privacy settings page. Uninstalling
JobFill also removes its storage from your browser. Because we never receive your data, we have
nothing to delete on our side.

## Permissions

- **storage:** to keep your profile on your device.
- **Access to websites** (Chrome shows "Read and change all your data on all websites"): so
  JobFill can find and fill application forms on the sites you apply on, including forms embedded
  in other pages. It writes to a page only when you click Autofill or Insert Answer.

JobFill does not request access to your tabs list, browsing history, cookies or downloads.

## Children

JobFill is meant for people applying for jobs. It isn't directed at children under 13, and we
don't knowingly collect children's data. As explained above, we don't collect anyone's data.

## Changes to this policy

If JobFill's data handling changes (for example, if we ever offer a hosted AI service), we will
update this policy and the "Last updated" date before the change ships. If a change would send
data somewhere new, we will say so in the extension before anything is sent.
